Hi, I'm Harley

These days I shoot commercials, brand films and documentaries for production companies across Michigan. Weddings are where all of that comes together. They're unscripted, emotional, and they happen once. You don't get a second take on a first look.

I'm married to Kelsie (Intricate Exposures), and we have two little ones who make life a lot of fun. I know how much goes into a wedding day and how fast it goes by. My job is to slow it down for you, so years from now you can press play and be right back in it.

How booking works

Booking a videographer shouldn't be one more thing to stress about. Here's what happens from first message to final film.

  1. Say hello

    Send your date and venue. We'll reply within 24 hours with our availability and pricing guide.

  2. Hop on a call

    A relaxed 20-minute video chat so we can get to know each other and make sure we're a good fit.

  3. Lock in your date

    Sign the contract online and pay a retainer, and your date is reserved for you.

  4. Plan the story

    About a month out, we go over your timeline, the people who matter most, any letters or readings, and the music you love.

  5. Enjoy your day

    We show up early, blend in and film it all, so you can stay present.

  6. Watch it back

    Your teaser arrives within a week, followed by your full film in a private online gallery you can share with family.

Watch your wedding film before the night ends

Our same-night add-on: while you're on the dance floor, we're editing. Before the last dance, your guests gather around a big screen for a 2–4 minute film of everything that happened that day.

Requires two cinematographers and an on-site editor.

Questions couples ask us

Do we really need a videographer if we have a photographer?

Photos show what your day looked like. Film shows what it sounded and felt like: your voices, the laughter during the toasts, the song you walked down the aisle to.

Will you get in the way?

No. We use small gear and keep our distance during the ceremony, so it's easy to forget we're there.

Do you work with our photographer?

Always. We reach out to your photographer before the wedding so we can plan angles and stay out of each other's shots. Book photo and film together through Intricate Exposures and it's one team from start to finish.

Can we choose the music?

Yes. Tell us the songs and artists you love and we'll pick fully licensed tracks that match, so your film can be shared anywhere without getting muted.

Do you travel?

We're based in Grand Rapids and film all over Michigan, and we're happy to travel for destination weddings.

What if it rains?

Rain makes for some of the best footage. We bring weather-sealed gear and plan backup shots with you ahead of time.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend 9–18 months out. Popular Saturdays from June through October fill first.
