The day goes fast. The film stays.

Most of your wedding day will be a blur. The vows. Your dad's toast. The way your best friend lost it on the dance floor. We film all of it quietly, the way it actually happened, and turn it into a film you'll want to watch every anniversary.

Approach

Real moments, filmed like cinema.

We'll guide a few moments when it helps, then step back and let the day happen. You'll spend your wedding with your people, not posing for a camera.

i.

Real emotion first

The tears, the inside jokes, the chaos on the dance floor. The real moments are what make a wedding film, and we make sure they're all in yours.

ii.

Cinematic by trade

Commercial-grade cameras, lenses, audio and drone, run by someone who lights and frames for a living. Clean, true-to-life color that won't look dated in ten years.

iii.

Built around your story

I started out as a photographer, and I still see everything in frames: the light coming through a window, a hand on a shoulder, the half second before someone laughs. Moving into film let me catch what happens between those frames, too.

These days I shoot commercials, brand films and documentaries, filming and editing for production companies across Michigan. Weddings are where all of that comes together. They're unscripted, emotional, and they happen exactly once. You don't get a second take on a first look.

I'm married to Kelsie (Intricate Exposures), and we have two little ones who make life so much fun. I know how much goes into a wedding day, and how fast it goes by. My job is to slow it down for you, so years from now you can press play and be right back in it.

Every film gets held to the same standard. Whoever is behind the camera on your day, your film is shot and finished to the same cinematic standard, and you work with us directly from the first call to final delivery.

The Experience

How it works.

Booking a videographer shouldn't be one more thing to stress about. Here's exactly what happens.

  1. Say hello

    Send your date and venue. We'll reply within 24 hours with our availability and pricing guide.

  2. Hop on a call

    A relaxed 20-minute video chat so we can get to know each other and make sure we're a good fit.

  3. Lock in your date

    Sign the contract online and pay a retainer. Your date is now reserved for you alone.

  4. Plan the story

    About a month out, we go over your timeline, the people who matter most, any letters or readings, and the music you love.

  5. Live your day

    We show up early, blend in, and capture it all. You get to stay present.

  6. Watch it back

    Your teaser arrives within a week, followed by your full film, delivered to a private online gallery you can share with family.

Questions

Good to know.

Do we really need a videographer if we have a photographer?

Photos show what your day looked like. Film shows what it sounded and felt like: your voices, the laughter during the toasts, the song you walked down the aisle to. It's the thing couples most often say they wish they'd booked.

Will you get in the way?

No. We use small, quiet gear and keep our distance during the ceremony. Most couples tell us they forgot we were there.

Do you work with our photographer?

Always. We reach out to your photographer before the wedding so we can plan angles and stay out of each other's shots. Book photo and film together through Intricate Exposures and it's one team from start to finish.

Can we choose the music?

Yes. Tell us the songs and artists you love and we'll pick fully licensed tracks that match, so your film can be shared anywhere without getting muted.

Do you travel?

We're based in Grand Rapids and film all over Michigan, and we're happy to travel for destination weddings.

What if it rains?

Rain makes for some of the best footage. We bring weather-sealed gear and plan backup shots with you ahead of time.

How far in advance should we book?

Most couples book 9–18 months out. Popular Saturdays from June through October go first.
