I don't direct your day. I watch it closely and stay out of the way. You'll spend your wedding with your people, not posing for a camera.

i.

Hi, I'm Harley

Cinematographer. Former photographer. Husband and dad.

I started out as a photographer, and I still see everything in frames: the light coming through a church window, a hand on a shoulder, the half second before someone laughs. Moving into film let me catch what happens between those frames, too.

These days I shoot commercials, brand films and documentaries, and I edit for production companies across Michigan. Weddings are where all of that comes together. They're unscripted, emotional, and they happen exactly once. You don't get a second take on a first look.

I'm married to Kelsie, and we have two little kids who keep life loud. I know how much goes into a wedding day, and how fast it goes by. My job is to slow it down for you, so years from now you can press play and be right back in it.

When you book with me, you get me. I film your wedding myself and I edit your film myself. I never send an associate in my place, and I never hand your footage to an editor you've never met.

The Experience

How it works.

Booking a videographer shouldn't be one more thing to stress about. Here's exactly what happens.

  1. Say hello

    Send your date and venue. I'll reply within 24 hours with my availability and pricing guide.

  2. Hop on a call

    A relaxed 20-minute video chat so we can get to know each other and make sure we're a good fit.

  3. Lock in your date

    Sign the contract online and pay a retainer. Your date is now reserved for you alone.

  4. Plan the story

    About a month out, we go over your timeline, the people who matter most, any letters or readings, and the music you love.

  5. Live your day

    I show up early, blend in, and capture it all. You get to stay present.

  6. Watch it back

    Your teaser arrives within a week, followed by your full film, delivered to a private online gallery you can share with family.

Questions

Good to know.

Do we really need a videographer if we have a photographer?

Photos show what your day looked like. Film shows what it sounded and felt like: your voices, the laughter during the toasts, the song you walked down the aisle to. It's the thing couples most often say they wish they'd booked.

Will you get in the way?

No. I use small, quiet gear and keep my distance during the ceremony. Most couples tell me they forgot I was there.

Do you work with our photographer?

Always. I reach out to your photographer before the wedding so we can plan angles and stay out of each other's shots. Book photo and film together through Intricate Exposures and it's one team from start to finish.

Can we choose the music?

Yes. Tell me the songs and artists you love and I'll pick fully licensed tracks that match, so your film can be shared anywhere without getting muted.

Do you travel?

I'm based in Grand Rapids and film all over Michigan, and I'm happy to travel for destination weddings.

What if it rains?

Rain makes for some of the best footage. I bring weather-sealed gear and plan backup shots with you ahead of time.

How far in advance should we book?

Most couples book 9–18 months out. Popular Saturdays from June through October go first.
